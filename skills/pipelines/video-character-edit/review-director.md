# Review Director - Video Character Edit

Generate matched-timestamp contact sheets for the source, outfit, hair, and
face outputs. Review each stage independently for:

- identity and expression stability
- outfit and hair adherence
- body motion and timing
- camera and background drift
- segment seams and frame flicker
- original audio presence and duration

Automatic scores are advisory only until they have been calibrated against
human-reviewed examples. A contact sheet with no manual decision is
`REVIEW_REQUIRED`, not `PASS`.

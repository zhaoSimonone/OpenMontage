# Story Plan Director - Narrative Short Pipeline

## Goal

Convert the approved brief into one emotionally complete story told by exactly
two generated 15-second clips.

## Planning Rules

- Establish the situation within the first two seconds.
- Give each segment one primary action, one readable emotional shift, and one
  terminal state.
- Keep one character and one location family. A location change is allowed only
  when the bridge prop makes the cause and effect clear.
- Avoid a seam in the middle of a turn, embrace, fast reach, or complex hand
  movement.
- In male POV stories, express the male lead only through camera, off-screen
  voice, breathing, and environmental interaction. Never plan a visible male
  body part.

## Required Artifacts

Write schema-valid `script` and `scene_plan`. The script uses approved dialogue
and narration text; scene directions describe visible performance separately.
